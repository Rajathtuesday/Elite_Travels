What we collect when you use this website, and what we do with it.

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy covers elitetravels.in - how the enquiry form, WhatsApp links and this site's third-party services (Web3Forms, Google Maps, Google Fonts) handle your information. It does not cover data collected offline, over the phone, or through your Google account when you interact with our Google Business listing directly.

What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form on our Contact page, we collect exactly what you type into it: your name, phone number, the service you're asking about, and any message you add. Nothing is collected silently in the background - this site runs no analytics and sets no tracking cookies of its own.

How we use it

Your enquiry is used for one purpose: to get back to you about the trip you're asking about, by phone, WhatsApp or email. We don't sell, rent or share your enquiry details with anyone outside Elite International Travels, except the service providers below who help us receive and process it.

Third-party services this site uses

  • Web3Forms - processes and delivers the Contact page form to our inbox. Your form submission passes through their servers; see their own privacy policy for how they handle that in transit.
  • Google Maps - the map embedded on our Contact page is loaded directly from Google, subject to Google's own privacy policy. Google may set cookies or collect usage data when that embed loads, independent of anything we do.
  • Google Fonts - this site loads typefaces from Google's font servers, which may log your IP address as part of that request.
  • WhatsApp - the WhatsApp and "Call Us" buttons hand off to WhatsApp's and your phone's own apps; any conversation you start there is governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy, not this one.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to plan and follow up on your trip, and are deleted on request - see "Your rights" below.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Contact us using the details above and we'll act on it promptly.

Changes to this policy

If how we handle your data changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
